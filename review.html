#1 SPOTLIGHT

Sahil "Omega" Jakhar

IGL & Captain Co-Owner Joined Aug 3, 2026

Sahil "Omega" Jakhar is widely regarded as one of Indian BGMI's sharpest tactical minds, blending veteran macro-leadership with an aggressive, high-ceiling roster at Team Outrage.

Lesser-Known Facts & IGL Pedigree

  • Player-Owner Double Duty: Beyond serving as Team Outrage's captain and In-Game Leader (IGL) since the roster formed on August 3, 2026, Omega is also listed as an Owner of the organization—making him one of the rare active Tier-1 Indian IGLs calling shots both inside the server and at the ownership level.
  • Started as a "Healer" Underdog: Long before becoming a household name with Team SouL, Omega competed under the tag Max Alpha at the PUBG Mobile India Series 2019, where his squad actually earned the "The Healers" tournament award rather than a fragging title.
  • The "Roster Architect" Reputation: Unlike IGLs who simply plug into pre-built superteams, Omega has a track record of hand-crafting championship cores from scratch. He famously scouted and built the Team SouL core alongside Akshat during their Chemin Esports days, later revitalized K9 Esports into BGMS Season 4 champions, and reunited with former K9 teammates Slug and NinjaBoi (alongside Knight and JDGamingYT) to form Team Outrage.
  • Micro-Management & Zone Control: Omega’s IGLing is built on disciplined early rotations and strict role delegation. By absorbing low-ego support and flank-watching duties himself, he creates space for heavy assaulters like NinjaBoi (1.65 FPM) and Slug (1.23 FPM) to take isolated 1v1s, allowing Team Outrage to post explosive single-day surges (such as dropping 30 finishes in a single day at BMIC 2026).
