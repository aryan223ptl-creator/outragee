IGL • Team Captain & Owner • The Mastermind

Sahil "Omega" Jakhar

The tactical backbone, In-Game Leader (IGL), and Owner of Team Outrage. Born February 24, 2000, Omega is widely regarded as one of the sharpest macro minds and most resilient captains in Indian esports history—having led two completely different generations of rosters (Team SouL and K9 Esports) to official Tier-1 BGMI national championships while representing India on the global stage.

Unknown & Lesser-Known Facts

Fact 01 • Army Family Roots
Frequent Childhood Relocations Shaped His IGL Comms

Growing up in an army-connected family, Omega frequently moved across cities during his childhood. Constantly adapting to new schools and friend circles built the calm communication, adaptability, and leadership instincts that define his late-zone shot-calling today.

Fact 02 • Dual Role Pioneer
Both Active IGL and Owner of Team Outrage

When Team Outrage launched on August 3, 2026, Omega didn't just step in as the squad's In-Game Leader—he officially took charge as the Owner and Team Captain, building a player-first home for NinjaBoi, Slug, Knight, JDGamingYT, and Coach Amit.

Fact 03 • Multi-Era Champion IGL
Won Major Titles 3+ Years Apart With Different Squads

Very few Indian IGLs have conquered multiple eras of BGMI. Omega captained Team SouL to the BMPS Season 1 trophy in June 2022, then rebuilt from scratch to captain K9 Esports to the BGMI Masters Series (BGMS) Season 4 championship in September 2025.

Fact 04 • Grassroots Architect
Built His Own Squad From Scratch in 2019

After failing to qualify in his very first tournament, Omega realized raw aim alone wasn't enough without structure. He founded Team Maximus Alpha from the ground up and shocked the circuit by reaching #7 in the PUBG Mobile India Series (PMIS) 2019 Grand Finals.

Fact 05 • The "Team Omega" Legacy
His Unsponsored Banner Became a Launchpad

Before signing with major organizations—and again during roster transitions in 2024—Omega competed under his self-titled Team Omega banner, even capturing 1st place at The World Of Battle: Winter Invitational (2024) while unsponsored.

Fact 06 • Unrivaled Longevity
Defied Every "Era Over" Narrative Since 2019

Across the 2020 game ban, high-profile roster splits at Team SouL and Carnival Gaming, and multiple resets, Omega has reached the Grand Finals of every major Indian LAN circuit from PMIS 2019 through BMIC and CEGC 2026—earning respect as India's ultimate comeback IGL.

Unknown & Lesser-Known Facts

Fact 01 • Unmatched Efficiency
Highest FPM in the All-Time Top 10

While ranked #4 overall in all-time Indian BGMI LAN finishes (928 kills), NinjaBoi holds the highest Finishes-Per-Match rate (1.59 FPM) among everyone in the Top 10—outpacing players with hundreds more matches played.

Fact 02 • Triple Crown Sweep
Three Individual Awards in One Event

At BGIS 2024 with Global Esports, he didn't just win MVP and Most Finishes—he also swept the Rampage Award on the exact same day (June 30, 2024), taking home over $5,398 in solo bonus prizes alone.

Fact 03 • Rapid LAN Scaling
Nearly 5x LAN Kill Jump in Three Years

His LAN output escalated every single season without a dip—jumping from 86 LAN finishes in 2023 to 105 in 2024, tripling to 314 in 2025, and surging to 423 LAN finishes in 2026.

Fact 04 • Brief Underdog Stint
Played Under "Team Omega" Before K9

Between leaving Global Esports in October 2024 and officially signing with K9 Esports in November 2024, NinjaBoi briefly competed under the unsponsored banner Team Omega in 1M Dynasty and Deepavali BattleMania.

Fact 05 • Clutch Specialist
Official BMPS 2025 Best Clutch Winner

Beyond pure entry fragging, NinjaBoi proved his late-zone composure by winning the official Best Clutch individual award at the Battlegrounds Mobile India Pro Series (BMPS) 2025.

Fact 06 • Podium Consistency
27 Podiums Across 130+ Tournaments

Across 130 published tournaments, he has secured 27 podium finishes (top 3)—meaning he finishes on the podium in more than 1 out of every 5 events he enters.

Playing as an assaulter for Team Outrage alongside Omega, NinjaBoi, and Knight, Slug has competed across Team iNSANE, Global Esports, K9 Esports, Divine Gaming, and Team Outrage—cementing his place among India's top 15 all-time LAN fraggers.

Unknown & Lesser-Known Facts

Fact 01 • Unbreakable Core
Four Straight Orgs With NinjaBoi

Slug and NinjaBoi form one of BGMI's longest-running assaulter duos—having moved together across four consecutive rosters: Global Esports, K9 Esports, Divine Gaming, and now Team Outrage.

Fact 02 • Shared Crown
Tied #1 Finisher at ESL Snapdragon S6

At ESL Snapdragon Pro Series Season 6 (2025), Slug matched teammate NinjaBoi to finish as Finisher #1 while also earning the Most WWCD #2 individual recognition in the same event.

Fact 03 • 82% Career LAN Output
Over Four-Fifths of LAN Kills in Two Years

Out of his 723 all-time LAN finishes, 597 kills (over 82%) came during his 2025–2026 surge (274 in 2025 and 323 in 2026), jumping more than 7x from his 45 LAN finishes in 2023.

Fact 04 • Knockout Specialist
Back-to-Back Elimination Stage MVPs

Before claiming major finals hardware, Slug built a reputation for thriving under elimination pressure—winning Semi-Finals MVP at Upthrust Challengers Showdown S2 and Eliminator Week MVP at Upthrust India Rising in 2024.

Fact 05 • Top-15 LAN Club
#14 All-Time LAN Finisher in India

With 723 finishes across 553 LAN matches (1.31 FPM), Slug sits at #14 in Indian BGMI LAN history—giving Team Outrage two active Top-15 all-time LAN fraggers on the same starting roster.

Fact 06 • Finals Peaker
Crowned BMPS 2026 Finals MVP

Delivering his biggest career individual accolade on the grandest stage, Slug captured the BMPS 2026 Finals MVP award after leading his squad through the championship lobby.

Playing as an assaulter for Team Outrage alongside Omega, NinjaBoi, Slug, and JDGamingYT, Knight broke into Tier-1 BGMI in 2025 and has rapidly climbed the statistical leaderboards across Team H4K, NoNx Esports, K9 Esports, Divine Gaming, and Team Outrage.

Team H4K NoNx Esports K9 Esports Divine Gaming Team Outrage (Present)

Unknown & Lesser-Known Facts

Fact 01 • Rapid Top-45 Entry
All-Time #44 LAN Rank in Just Two Seasons

Despite only breaking into Tier-1 BGMI in 2025, Knight has already racked up 440 LAN finishes in 368 matches (1.20 FPM)—cracking India's All-Time Top 45 LAN leaderboard faster than almost any newcomer of his generation.

Fact 02 • 3.78x Sophomore Explosion
79% of All Career LAN Kills Came in 2026

After posting 92 LAN finishes during his 2025 rookie Tier-1 year, Knight nearly quadrupled his LAN output in 2026 with 348 finishes—accounting for 79% of his all-time LAN tally.

Fact 03 • Ironclad Domestic Floor
Never Below 1.38 FPM in Any 2026 Domestic Major

Across 266 major 2026 tournament matches (388 total finishes at 1.46 FPM), Knight never dipped below a 1.38 FPM average in a single Indian domestic major—spanning BGIS, BMPS, BGMS, BMSD, and CEGC.

Fact 04 • Marathon Fragging
263 Finishes Across BGMS & BMSD 2026 Alone

In two high-volume 2026 tournaments—BGMI Masters Series (153 finishes in 106 matches) and BGMI Showdown (110 finishes in 70 matches at 1.57 FPM)—Knight delivered 263 kills in 176 games.

Fact 05 • Peak Form Entering Outrage
Career-Best 1.58 FPM Pace at CEGC 2026

In the ongoing Chennai Esports Global Championship (CEGC) 2026, Knight is operating at a tournament-best 1.58 FPM (19 finishes in 12 matches)—his sharpest scoring efficiency of the 2026 calendar.

Fact 06 • Three-Org Core
Teamed With NinjaBoi & Slug Across Three Banners

Following early stints with Team H4K and NoNx Esports, Knight linked up with NinjaBoi and Slug at K9 Esports, carried the core through Divine Gaming, and reunited under Team Outrage.
